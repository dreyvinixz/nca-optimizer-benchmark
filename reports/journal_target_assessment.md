# Provisional journal target assessment

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-26

**Working target:** *Journal of Forecasting* (Wiley), regular research article track.
**Status:** provisional internal choice; no submission has been made.

## Fit rationale

The journal's official scope is broad across theoretical, practical, computational, and methodological forecasting, including applications in business and technology. This project is a computational study of predicting intraday market labels, with a chronological holdout and explicit comparison of forecasting models. That is a plausible scope match.

The fit is not assured. The target is a retrospective B0 label, not a market return, and the study does not establish economic utility. The modeling contribution is a compact comparison rather than a new forecasting method. The paper should retain the precise label-reproduction framing; the editor may judge the empirical contribution too narrow.

## Verified author requirements

The current Wiley author guide states that the journal accepts free-format submissions and requests a readable editable manuscript with abstract, main body, references, and tables; references may initially use any consistent style. Its detailed manuscript guidance specifies APA references, up to six keywords, a title without abbreviations, and a running title under 40 characters. The title page requests full author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and applicable ethics and integrity statements. ORCID is required. The journal operates single-blind review and expects data sharing, with Wiley's policy allowing an explanation when data cannot be shared for legal or confidentiality reasons.
