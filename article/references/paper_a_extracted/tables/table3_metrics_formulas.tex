\caption{Quantitative metrics for assessing accuracy, computational efficiency, and financial viability of neural models.}

\centering
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L l L@{}}
\toprule
Metric & Abbreviation & Mathematical Formula \\
\midrule
Accuracy & ACC & $\frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN}$ \\
$F_1$-Score & $F_1$ & $\frac{2 \cdot TP}{2 \cdot TP + FP + FN}$ \\
Matthews Correlation Coefficient & MCC & $\frac{TP \cdot TN - FP \cdot FN}{\sqrt{(TP+FP)(TP+FN)(TN+FP)(TN+FN)}}$ \\
Area Under ROC Curve & AUC-ROC & $\int_0^1 \operatorname{TPR}(t) \, d\operatorname{FPR}(t)$ \\
Net Trading Return & Return (\%) & $\left( \prod_{k=1}^{N_{\text{trades}}} (1 + r_k - c_{\text{fee}}) - 1 \right) \times 100$ \\
Sharpe Ratio & Sharpe & $\frac{\mathbb{E}[R_{\text{daily}} - R_f]}{\sigma_{\text{daily}}} \cdot \sqrt{252}$ \\
Maximum Drawdown & MDD (\%) & $\max_{\tau \le t} \left( \frac{V_\tau - V_t}{V_\tau} \right) \times 100$ \\
\botrule
\end{tabularx}
