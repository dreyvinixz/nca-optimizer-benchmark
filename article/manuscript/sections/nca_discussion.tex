\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

This section interprets the results in relation to the methodological sequence established by the IJCNN precursor. The precursor showed that a compact technical-indicator representation and GA-based tuning could produce useful short-term trend predictions. The present benchmark preserves that foundation and tests which conclusions remain valid when the data split, search budget, optimizer family, fitness criterion, and economic endpoint are controlled explicitly.

\subsection{Methodological Continuity and Extension}

The continuity between the two studies is visible in four stages: the same B3 intraday forecasting problem, construction of short-window technical indicators, Information Gain reduction to seven descriptors, and evolutionary hyperparameter search. The extension is not a replacement of that pipeline, but a stricter experimental comparison. Instead of a randomly sampled training subset and a single GA, the present design retains chronological order, evaluates RF, SVM, MLP, and 1D-CNN, compares RS, GA, PSO, DE, and GWO under 1,000 candidate fits, and keeps the final test block isolated.

\subsection{Predictive Performance and Fitness Choice}

The descriptive means show a small advantage for the compound MCC/$F_1$ protocol in both macro test accuracy (0.623 versus 0.617) and macro test MCC (0.247 versus 0.235). This difference should not be interpreted as universal superiority. The configuration heatmaps contain both positive and negative cells, demonstrating that the selected fitness interacts with the backbone and optimizer. In particular, optimizing validation accuracy does not guarantee higher held-out accuracy, while an imbalance-aware criterion does not dominate every individual configuration.

The within-protocol statistical results provide a more stable conclusion: model identity matters under the controlled blocks. MLP obtains the best mean rank under the accuracy protocol and differs significantly from the other backbones in the exploratory paired comparisons. This finding is consistent with the precursor's observation that the MLP can represent nonlinear intraday boundaries, but it is now bounded by the common temporal split and evaluation budget.

\subsection{Optimizer Search Dynamics}

The model-specific convergence figures make the optimizer comparison more informative than a final score alone. They show the search trajectories in the same style as the precursor's GA-fitness plots while placing every method on the common candidate-evaluation axis. Early improvement, late improvement, and trajectory dispersion can therefore be interpreted as search behaviour rather than as consequences of unequal generation or iteration counts. No optimizer is declared universally best because the relevant trajectory depends on both the backbone and the fitness landscape.

\subsection{Economic Meaning of the Predictions}

The economic results confirm that predictive and trading rankings are related but not interchangeable. RF obtains the largest mean profit under MCC/$F_1$ fitness, whereas MLP obtains the largest mean profit under accuracy fitness. RF nevertheless has the least negative mean drawdown under the latter protocol. Transaction costs and the number of executed signals therefore alter the practical ordering that would be obtained from accuracy alone. A deployment decision must state whether profit, downside risk, turnover, or stability is the primary criterion.

\subsection{Limitations and Future Work}

The direct dual-fitness contrast is descriptive because only three seeds are common to every model--optimizer cell. The Friedman and Wilcoxon analyses are exploratory model-level tests and do not represent independent market periods. The dataset covers one liquid Brazilian futures contract and one chronological test segment, so transfer to other instruments and regimes remains untested. Finally, the fixed 1,000-evaluation cap establishes budget parity but does not characterize asymptotic behaviour at larger computational budgets.

The next phase should retain the same IJCNN-style sequence and run a preregistered common 20-seed study for every cell. Each candidate evaluation should be archived with validation MCC, accuracy, $F_1$, and economic statistics, followed by paired inference performed separately for each endpoint. This would provide confirmatory evidence while preserving the leakage-free and equal-budget design established here.
