\section{Related Work \& Literature Gap Matrix}\label{sec:related_work}

This section synthesizes recent advances in financial machine learning, technical indicator engineering, and metaheuristic hyperparameter optimization, highlighting the core research gaps addressed by this benchmark study.

\subsection{Financial Forecasting \& Technical Indicator Engineering}\label{sec:rel_financial}

Financial price forecasting in high-frequency derivative markets has long been a benchmark domain for computational intelligence \cite{billah2024nca_moving_average, bashir2026evobagnet}. High-frequency financial time series exhibit high noise, non-linear dynamics, and rapid market regime shifts \cite{cardoso2022bovdb}. Traditional statistical forecasting methods—such as Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) and Linear Regression—struggle with non-linear volatility spikes \cite{henrique2019stock_review}. Consequently, researchers have adopted Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL) models, including Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs), Random Forests, XGBoost, and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks \cite{ecer2020mlp_ga_pso, sekhar2025stock}.

To feed these machine learning architectures, technical indicators—such as Simple Moving Averages (SMA), Exponential Moving Averages (EMA), Standard Deviation (STDDEV), Relative Strength Index (RSI), and Bollinger Bands—are engineered from raw Open-High-Low-Close (OHLC) candles \cite{bollinger1992using, rahim2025technical}. However, constructing large feature sets introduces high dimensionality and multi-collinear noise. Feature selection techniques—such as Information Gain (IG), Stability-Enhanced Dynamic Thresholding (SEDT), and ReliefF—are applied to filter out redundant attributes, isolating high-impact trend and volatility signals \cite{souza2025iccsa, mutinda2026hybrid}.

\subsection{Metaheuristic Hyperparameter Tuning in Neural Models}\label{sec:rel_metaheuristics}

While neural architectures can approximate complex continuous mappings, backpropagation standard gradient descent optimizes internal connection weights given a fixed network structure. Performance remains acutely sensitive to hyperparameter selection \cite{dhingra2025solar, al_bannoud2026accelerating}. Manual trial-and-error, Grid Search, and Random Search are computationally inefficient or unguided when searching high-dimensional discrete-continuous search spaces \cite{bergstra2012random_search}.

Metaheuristic Optimization Algorithms (MOAs)—encompassing Evolutionary Computation (e.g., Genetic Algorithms and Differential Evolution) and Swarm Intelligence (e.g., Particle Swarm Optimization and Grey Wolf Optimizer)—have emerged as effective gradient-free search engines \cite{goldberg1989ga, kennedy1995pso, storn1997de, faris2018gwo_review}. In recent medical and industrial benchmarks, surrogate-assisted metaheuristic optimization (such as ANN–GWO and ANN–PSO) demonstrated over 99\% computational time savings over numerical differential solvers while maintaining high accuracy \cite{al_bannoud2026accelerating}. Similarly, in stock market forecasting, evolutionary ensembles (such as EvoBagNet) achieved superior generalization by adaptively tuning model hyperparameters \cite{bashir2026evobagnet}.

\subsection{Validation Protocols, Budget Equality, and Temporal Leakage Risks}\label{sec:rel_gaps}

Despite widespread adoption of metaheuristics, published literature suffers from systemic validation flaws:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Unequal Evaluation Budgets}: Prior studies frequently compare an evolutionary algorithm against a swarm optimizer without controlling the total count of candidate function evaluations \cite{dhingra2025solar, rajwar2023exhaustive_review}. Algorithms granted higher evaluation counts naturally explore wider regions of the search space.
    \item \textbf{Look-Ahead Data Leakage}: Randomized $K$-fold cross-validation is frequently applied to chronological time series \cite{souza2026ijcnn, simatupang2026stock}. Shuffling intraday bars breaks temporal ordering ($T_{\text{train}} < T_{\text{val}} < T_{\text{test}}$), allowing future price information to leak into training sets.
    \item \textbf{Classification vs. Economic Disconnect}: Optimizers are tuned on raw classification accuracy or Mean Squared Error without testing out-of-sample trading viability under realistic exchange fees and slippage \cite{chicco2020mcc, bareket2024adaptive}.
\end{enumerate}
