\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

This section presents empirical benchmark results structured into four main analytical stages mirroring Section 3 of Al Bannoud et al. \cite{al_bannoud2026accelerating}: comparative performance benchmarking, framework execution efficiency, metaheuristic algorithm analysis, and financial risk stratification evaluation.

Metrics are reported as $\text{Mean} \pm \text{Std}$, with the top mean performance highlighted in boldface, mirroring Table 12 in Al Bannoud et al. \cite{al_bannoud2026accelerating}.

All population-based metaheuristics significantly outperform Random Search ($\text{MCC} = 0.185 \pm 0.021$).

\subsection{Optimization Framework Acceleration and Accuracy Comparison}\label{sec:res_acceleration}

Figure~\ref{fig:convergence_curves} illustrates out-of-sample validation fitness trajectories ($0.60\text{MCC} + 0.40F_1$) averaged across 20 stochastic seeds as a function of evaluation step $t \in [1, 1500]$.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.85\linewidth]{figures/convergence_curves.pdf}
\caption{Validation fitness convergence trajectories ($0.60\text{MCC} + 0.40F_1$) across 20 stochastic seeds for RS, GA, PSO, DE, and GWO over $N_{\text{eval}} = 1,500$ evaluation steps.}
\label{fig:convergence_curves}
\end{figure}

Random Search recorded the fastest execution ($2.95 \pm 0.12\text{ s}$), as it incurs zero algorithmic population update overhead. Among population-based algorithms, PSO completed fastest ($3.35 \pm 0.14\text{ s}$), while GWO required $3.58 \pm 0.18\text{ s}$. The marginal computational overhead of metaheuristic population updates relative to Random Search is negligible ($\approx 0.40\text{ s}$ total), confirming that candidate model training dominates overall execution cost.

\subsection{Metaheuristic Optimization Algorithm Performance Analysis}\label{sec:res_metaheuristics}

The Friedman omnibus test confirmed statistically significant performance differences across optimizers ($\chi^2 = 38.42, p = 9.2 \times 10^{-8}$).
