\section{Related Work}\label{sec:relatedwork}

The use of machine learning and deep learning for financial prediction has expanded as public and proprietary market datasets have become more accessible. Financial series remain challenging because apparent regularities may be temporary and because predictive evaluation can be distorted by an inappropriate temporal split \cite{henrique2019stock_review,rajwar2023exhaustive_review}. Studies comparing learning algorithms therefore commonly report that model choice, feature representation, and validation design jointly affect the observed ranking.

Technical analysis indicators are a common bridge between raw market prices and supervised models. Moving-average and volatility descriptors summarize short-run trend and dispersion, but a large indicator set may introduce redundancy and unstable fitted models. Information-theoretic filtering is a simple and reproducible route to retain attributes that reduce uncertainty about the target class \cite{lei2012feature}. The precursor adopted this strategy to reduce 66 engineered descriptors to seven inputs before fitting RF, SVM, and MLP classifiers \cite{souza2026ijcnn}.

Hyperparameter optimization is equally important when neural, kernel, and ensemble models are applied to financial data. GA provides a population-based global search mechanism; PSO exploits individual and collective attraction; DE uses differential perturbations; and GWO uses a hierarchy of leading agents \cite{goldberg1989ga,kennedy1995pso,storn1997de,faris2018gwo_review}. RS is also necessary in a controlled comparison because it supplies a transparent budget-matched baseline \cite{bergstra2012random_search}. These methods cannot be compared fairly when one receives more candidate evaluations than another.

Recent financial-learning studies have also emphasized temporal evaluation and economic relevance. A validation score selected on one period need not transfer to a later regime, and classification accuracy alone cannot specify the return-risk trade-off of a trading rule. MCC is useful when class balance and both types of classification error matter, while trading evaluation additionally requires profit and drawdown measures \cite{chicco2020mcc,makridakis2018statistical}.

Unlike the precursor, the present work integrates feature selection, equal-budget multi-optimizer search, two validation fitness functions, locked chronological testing, and economic evaluation in one protocol. The extension is deliberately conservative: direct comparisons between the two fitness functions are descriptive because only three common seeds are available in every model--optimizer cell.
